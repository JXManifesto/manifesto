# JX Manifesto governance

JX is an open, versioned professional manifesto. Its governance is designed to keep decisions reviewable, preserve bilingual meaning, and prevent the working text from silently replacing stable public versions.

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** triages proposals, facilitates discussion, records decisions, coordinates bilingual review, and prepares releases. The current maintainer is Javid Momeni.
- **Contributor:** proposes text, principles, translations, research notes, case studies, design, or code. Accepted contributions retain individual attribution.
- **Reviewer:** tests clarity, evidence, translation, accessibility, or applicability from an affected perspective. Review does not require commit access.

The project should add maintainers when contributors have demonstrated sustained, constructive work across review and release cycles. Maintainer changes must be recorded in this file and `CONTRIBUTORS.md`.

## Decision path

1. **Draft:** a focused issue describes the current problem, proposed change, affected groups, evidence, alternatives, and conflicts of interest.
2. **Discussion:** affected people compare evidence, trade-offs, regional or linguistic implications, and possible harms in public.
3. **Accepted / Rejected / Deferred:** a maintainer records the decision and rationale. Acceptance does not change a stable version. Deferral is preferred when evidence or affected perspectives are insufficient.
4. **Released:** accepted changes become part of JX only when included in a Git tag, GitHub Release, changelog, permanent bilingual pages and versioned downloads, and a persistent Zenodo record with a DOI.

Routine spelling, broken-link, accessibility, and implementation corrections may use a pull request without a separate decision issue when they do not change meaning.

## Decision criteria

Substantive proposals are evaluated for:

- benefit or risk to editorial quality and the people doing editorial work;
- evidence and applicability to a real tool, workflow, policy, or decision;
- effects across newsroom sizes, languages, regions, resources, and risk levels;
- semantic alignment between language editions;
- consistency with JX scope, non-goals, license, and prior released decisions;
- reversibility and the cost of getting the decision wrong.

Consensus is preferred but not required. When consensus is not possible, the maintainer records the strongest objections and why one option was chosen. New evidence may reopen a decision through a new issue.

## Conflicts of interest

Maintainers and contributors must disclose relevant employment, financial, vendor, product, funding, or advocacy interests on substantive proposals. A person with a material conflict may contribute evidence and discussion but should not make the final decision alone. If all current maintainers are conflicted, the decision is deferred until an independent reviewer is identified.

No institutional owner or project sponsor is currently declared. Future sponsorship, grants, or partnerships must be listed here with their scope. Funding must not grant control over findings, contributor attribution, or manifesto decisions.

## Conduct, privacy, and contact

Public work should happen in GitHub issues and pull requests. Do not post confidential newsroom, source, security, employment, or health information. Sensitive conduct reports may be sent to **javid.momeni@gmail.com** with the subject `JX Code of Conduct` and follow `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`. If a report concerns the current maintainer, use the independent route **editorial@jxmanifesto.org** instead.

## خلاصه فارسی

JX منشوری باز و نسخه‌مند است. مسیر تصمیم شامل «پیش‌نویس»، «گفت‌وگو»، «پذیرفته/ردشده/به‌تعویق‌افتاده» و «منتشرشده» است. تغییر مهم فقط پس از ثبت دلیل عمومی، بازبینی معنایی دو زبان و قرارگرفتن در تگ، GitHub Release، تاریخچه تغییرات، صفحات و فایل‌های دائمی دوزبانه و رکورد پایدار Zenodo دارای DOI به موضع رسمی پروژه تبدیل می‌شود.

نگه‌دار و مشارکت‌کننده باید تعارض منافع مالی، شغلی، محصولی، فروشندگی یا مدنی مرتبط را افشا کنند. فرد دارای تعارض مهم می‌تواند در بحث مشارکت کند، اما نباید به‌تنهایی تصمیم نهایی بگیرد. درحال‌حاضر مالک سازمانی یا حامی مالی برای پروژه اعلام نشده است.
